Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Simple Daily Planner. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you about how we look after your personal data when you use our mobile application and tell you about your privacy rights.

Information We Collect

We are committed to minimal data collection and maximum privacy. Here's what you should know about our data practices:

  • Local Storage: All planning data, including tasks, events, calendar entries, app preferences, and settings, is stored exclusively on your device. We do not have access to this information, and it never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it.
  • Analytics: We use Mixpanel solely to count users without identifying anything about individual users. Mixpanel does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, planning data, tasks, events, or any other user content.
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information. We don't require accounts, email addresses, or any other personal information to use the app.

Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party service:

  • Mixpanel: We use Mixpanel solely to count the number of users without identifying or collecting any personal information about individual users. All analytics data is anonymized and cannot be used to identify individual users. No planning data, tasks, events, or user content is transmitted to Mixpanel.

How We Use Your Information

Our data usage is minimal and transparent:

  • Analytics: We use Mixpanel solely to count users without identifying anything about individual users. This helps us understand basic usage metrics.
  • Local Data: All data stored on your device is used only to provide the app's core functionality. This includes:
    • Storing your tasks and events
    • Managing your calendar entries
    • Storing your preferences and settings
    • Maintaining your local app data
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not share any data with third parties, except for anonymized user count data sent to Mixpanel, which cannot identify individual users.

Data Security

Your privacy and data security are our top priorities:

  • All planning data is stored locally on your device using secure storage systems
  • We use secure infrastructure for analytics data
  • We implement industry-standard security measures to protect any data in transit
  • You have complete control over your data - you can delete the app at any time to remove all local data

Your Data Protection Rights

Since we store all planning data locally on your device, you have complete control over your information:

  • You can delete the app at any time to remove all local planning data
  • You can delete individual tasks and events at any time
  • Analytics data is collected anonymously through Mixpanel solely to count users. This data cannot be linked to individual users and is used in aggregate form only.
  • If you have any concerns about your privacy or data usage, please contact us through the methods listed below.

Analytics and Data Collection

We use Mixpanel for analytics purposes:

  • Mixpanel is used solely to count users without identifying anything about individual users
  • All analytics data is collected anonymously
  • We cannot identify individual users from the analytics data
  • No planning data (tasks, events, calendar entries) is transmitted to Mixpanel
  • No personal information is collected or transmitted
  • Analytics data is used in aggregate form only

Children's Privacy

Our service is designed for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. All planning data (tasks, events, calendar entries) is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to us or any third party (except for anonymized user count data sent to Mixpanel, which cannot identify individual users).

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
